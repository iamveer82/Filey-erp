# Critical reading

From the scientific-critical-thinking and peer-review skills in [Scientific Agent Skills](https://github.com/K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills) (MIT).

1. Read the paper with `read_web_page` or the attached file. If you cannot open it, say so.
2. State the claim, the comparison, the sample, and the result in the paper's own numbers.
3. Name one limit: what was not measured, or what the design cannot show.
4. Do not upgrade an association into a cause, and do not invent a p-value.
