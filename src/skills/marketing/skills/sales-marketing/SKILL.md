# Sales and launch

Covers prospecting, revops, sales-enablement, competitors, competitor-profiling, pricing, launch, directory-submissions, public-relations, and events from [Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) (MIT).

1. Prospects: `find_prospects` or `search_web`, then `create_lead` only for people or companies you can name from a result. No invented emails.
2. Competitors: `read_web_page` on their sites. A comparison states what you read. Pricing advice shows options and the tradeoff; the user picks the price.
3. A launch or one-pager is a `word` or `slides` draft: audience, promise, proof, and the ask.
4. Press, directories, and events are a list with the submission still unsent until the user approves.
