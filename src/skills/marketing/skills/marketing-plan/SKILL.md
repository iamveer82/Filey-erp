# Marketing plan

Covers marketing-plan, marketing-ideas, marketing-psychology, marketing-council, and customer-research from [Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) (MIT).

1. Load the product context. Customer research uses `search_web`, `read_web_page`, and `read_social_page` on places the buyers talk. Label quotes you did not find as hypotheses.
2. A plan is a few bets: channel, audience, offer, the Filey tool that runs it, and what "working" means. `update_plan` when the user wants it carried out.
3. Ideas stay tied to the product. Psychology is a reason to change a headline or an offer, not a trick.
4. When the user wants several angles, give three short views (acquisition, conversion, retention) and one recommendation. Then do the piece they pick with the matching skill.
