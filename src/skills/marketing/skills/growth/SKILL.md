# Growth

Covers referrals, churn-prevention, community-marketing, lead-magnets, free-tools, co-marketing, influencer-marketing, and marketing-loops from [Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) (MIT).

1. Name the loop: who invites whom, what they get, and what Filey can actually run (a campaign, a template, a reminder).
2. A lead magnet is a draft the user can send, saved with `word` or `save_email_template`. Do not promise a tool you did not build.
3. A churn save is an offer and a short message, plus `add_reminder` for the follow-up. Do not change a subscription or issue a refund unless the user asked and the matching Filey tool exists.
4. Partnerships and creators stay a shortlist with a reason. Outreach is a draft, not a sent message, until the user says to send it.
