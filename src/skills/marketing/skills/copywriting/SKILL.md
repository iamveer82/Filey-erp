# Copywriting

Covers the copywriting, copy-editing, and offers skills from [Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) (MIT).

1. `use_skill` product-marketing if `recall` has no product context.
2. Name the page and the one action the reader should take.
3. Draft headline, subhead, proof, offer, and the call to action. One idea per line. No invented testimonials, numbers, or customer names.
4. For a long page, save the draft with `word`. Editing means mark what changed and why, then give the revised copy.
5. Sending or publishing waits for the user.
