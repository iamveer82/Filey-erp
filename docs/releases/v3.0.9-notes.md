# Filey ERP 3.0.9

This update brings together the unpublished 3.0.8 fixes and the latest workspace improvements.

- Teams has private chats alongside shared channels, threaded replies and unread counts. Send up to five images or documents per message, up to 10 MB each, with private workspace and conversation access.
- Invite teammates with a six-character code or an email invitation. Owners and administrators approve join requests and manage roles and app access. Choose simple avatar presets for yourself and team members.
- Cloud workspaces support up to 20 registered devices. Device management and the device-limit screen now use **Log out**, with a clearer mobile layout. The separate Ultra offline activation allowance remains two devices.
- Basic includes unlimited local invoices. The separate Basic web allowance remains five invoices a month.
- Fixes address concurrent CRM saves, integration retries and quotas, upload-name collisions, CSV imports, incomplete record queries, and native command timeouts.

- Company settings and document forms use your country's visible field names: GSTIN, IFSC, State / Union territory and PIN code for India, with the relevant fields for other supported countries. Customer phone numbers support Indian and UAE local formats as well as international numbers.
- Country-aware labels extend to customers, suppliers, receipts, statements and tax summaries across desktop and web. New non-UAE declaration letters start with editable business wording. Saved documents retain their country and currency.
- Turn on cloud storage to upload pending device changes before switching. Turn it off to download cloud records and files before returning to this device. A failed transfer keeps your current workspace open and offers **Try again**.
- Desktop cloud downloads commit the workspace together. Unchanged records and files are reused to reduce repeat downloads.
- Mobile invoice previews fit the screen, with zoom available. Signup keeps the password chosen before email verification, including retries for unconfirmed accounts.
- Optional company registration documents are stored privately for safekeeping; changing country keeps existing identifiers and files.
- Invoice editing has focused Details, Items and Finishing touches tabs, templates at the top, visible totals and a collapsible live preview. Document section styling is consistent across invoices, quotations and purchase orders.
- Filey AI credits are now called Coin. 1 Coin remains $1; existing balances and transaction history are unchanged. Free OpenRouter models are hidden from the model picker and rate list, with API-key and local-model options retained.
- The sidebar uses consistent, locally bundled Material icons, while retaining Filey AI's animated icon.
- On mobile, swipe right from just inside the left edge to open navigation and left to close it. The drawer follows your finger and respects reduced-motion settings.
- CRM has a simpler toolbar, directly accessible task due-date filters and useful empty-state shortcuts. Closing a record with unsaved edits asks whether to keep editing or discard them. Mobile filters and tables remain readable.

- Shared checkboxes now use a consistent Filey style, including partial selection.
- Custom fields are available throughout CRM, with saved values visible in record details. Removing a field keeps its stored values.

Country labels do not convert the AED accounting ledger or provide statutory tax
returns. Internet access is required to finish cloud transfers.

## Downloads and updates

Windows installers are available as EXE and MSI. macOS has separate Apple Silicon
and Intel downloads; choose the architecture of your Mac. Linux packages are
available as DEB and RPM and must be updated by installing the latest package.
Windows and macOS auto-updater packages are signed with Filey's existing updater key.

The macOS app is not Apple-notarized, so macOS may show an unidentified-developer
notice. Review Apple's guidance for opening an app from an identified, trusted
source before installing. Updater signature verification is separate from Apple
notarization.
