# Filey ERP 3.0.7

- The web app no longer asks you to sign in a second time. Document templates, assistant history and saved CRM views failed to load on the web build with a "Sign in to this workspace" message even when you were already signed in, because the app never recorded which storage store it was using and every account-scoped lookup resolved to nothing.
- Settings → **Data and storage** is now one switch instead of two cards. Off keeps your records on this device only. Turning it **on** uploads them to your Filey account, so they follow you to every device you sign in to. Turning it **off** never uploads anything and does not delete anything from your account.
- Turning cloud storage on asks for your confirmation first, and uploads only the tables that actually hold records.
- Templates saved on a device before accounts existed can be restored. They were being kept but never loaded, which made them look lost. Use **Restore my templates** next to the template picker.
- The document preview now opens readable on a phone. It used to shrink the A4 page to fit the screen, leaving body text around 5 pixels tall; it now opens at a readable size and **Fit width** still shows the whole page. The page itself is never reflowed, so what you see is what prints and exports.
- Stamps and signatures now appear in PDFs downloaded from a phone. iOS drops images captured from the page, so they were missing from every exported invoice. They are now added to the file directly. Letterheads and other artwork are unaffected.

To move this device's records into your account, open Settings → **Data and storage** and turn on **Store in my Filey account**.
