# Filey ERP 3.0.16

Fixes local-to-cloud transfers where company settings were created independently
on the device and in the cloud. Settings are matched by name, with overlapping
IDs reconciled together so a bank, letterhead or numbering preference cannot
replace a different setting. Device-only settings remain preserved.

Large invoices and other documents now download individually during transfers
and conflict review. Embedded logos and signatures no longer accumulate into
one oversized request that can time out after uploads have already succeeded.

Includes the retired-workspace recovery and transfer error guidance prepared for
3.0.15, and the document, inventory, AI connection, profile mascot and workspace
file-transfer improvements prepared for 3.0.14. Those intermediate releases were
not published. See the [3.0.14 notes](v3.0.14-notes.md) for the earlier changes.

Ownership checks and revision checks remain enforced. Device mode stays open
until the transfer finishes. No new dependency or cloud migration is required.
